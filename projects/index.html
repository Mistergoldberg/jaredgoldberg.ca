💰 Projects

These projects are practical attempts to redesign the systems that shape everyday life.

They begin from a simple premise:

Many civic problems are not caused by a lack of care.

They are caused by weak infrastructure.

Financial literacy is taught too late.

Work is badly bundled.

Local value leaks out of communities.

People are asked to navigate systems they were never taught to read.

The projects here explore a different approach.

Build small.

Make the system visible.

Test the loop.

Improve the structure.

Some begin as essays.

Some become programs.

Some may become platforms.

Together, they are part of a larger body of work I think of as Capital Works: civic infrastructure designed around literacy, dignity, participation, and local economic resilience.

Not charity.

Architecture.

Not slogans.

Systems that hold.
