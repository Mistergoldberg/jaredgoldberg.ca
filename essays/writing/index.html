🧭 Writing

A series of essays exploring patterns that repeat across wealth, media, markets, biology, and belief.

Not arguments.

Instruments.

Ways of looking at familiar systems until they reveal the forces shaping them — compounding, incentives, feedback loops, hierarchy, and constraint.

The same logic appears in different costumes.

These essays trace those recurrences.

Not to persuade.

To make the machinery visible.
